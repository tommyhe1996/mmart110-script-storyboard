🎥 12.1 Camera Moves
📡 Types of Camera Moves
▶️ Track / Truck Shot
Camera physically moves sideways or forward/backward through space.
🔄 Pan
Camera rotates left ↔ right from a fixed position.
⤴️ Tilt
Camera rotates up ↕ down from one spot.
🔍 Zoom
Lens changes focal length; camera does not move.
Example (Taxi Driver):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=boKraRoVh5k
(Source: Columbia University Film Glossary)
🎭 Do You Need to Add Camera Moves?
Yes—only if motivated.
Ask:
What emotion does this movement create?
Why is the camera moving here?
Does the moment demand motion?
Camera movement = storytelling.
📐 How to Storyboard Camera Moves
Use:
Motion arrows
Multiple panels showing progression
Labels: PAN, TILT, TRACK, ZOOM
Notes under each panel
Combine framing + action + movement
Refer to the assigned chapter on storyboarding motion.
