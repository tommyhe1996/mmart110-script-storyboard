## Course Objectives
- **Generate Stories for Media**
  - Draw from personal experience, influences, imagination, and role play.
- **Structure Conventional Stories**
  - Build/relieve dramatic tension, create central characters, use dialogue effectively.
- **Format Scripts & Storyboards**
  - Pitch ideas, conceive micro-budget films, write industry-standard scripts.
- **Translate Across Media**
  - Film, radio, branded content, interactive formats.

**Final Deliverables**:  
- Portfolio-level **treatment + script**  
- Portfolio-level **storyboard** for a producible 3-minute film
