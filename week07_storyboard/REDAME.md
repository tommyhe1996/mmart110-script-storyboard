By the end of this week, you will be able to:

- 🎞️ Identify **shot sizes and framing**
- 🖼️ Learn **multiple methods of image creation**
- 🧭 Plan **how you will generate your storyboard**
- 🎥 Understand how storyboards support **production & post-production**

---

# 📘 Module Overview
The storyboard is your film’s **visual blueprint**.  
It:

- 📖 Maps the beginning–middle–end  
- 📈 Shows rising & falling tension  
- 🧩 Connects character action with cinematography  
- 🛠️ Gives every department clear visual direction  
- 🎞️ Guides editors and post-production artists  

You **do NOT need to be an artist**.  
Storyboards are about **communication**, not drawing perfection.

---

# 🎥 11.1 Storyboarding in the Filmmaking Process

Storyboarding helps you:

- 💡 Develop ideas *before* spending time & money on set  
- 🗣️ Communicate clearly with your team  
- ✂️ Provide guidance for editors and VFX in post  

Trying out ideas on set can waste hours.  
Storyboarding = **safe testing space**.

---

## 👥 Who Creates Storyboards?

### 🎬 **Director (YOU)**
Sets the visual language of the film.

### 🎥 **Director of Photography (DP)**
Uses storyboards to plan:

- Lensing  
- Framing  
- Lighting strategy  

### 🧑‍🎨 **Storyboard Artist** (optional)
More common in large productions.

👉 Canvas Resource: Interview with **Giuseppe Cristiano**, storyboard artist.

---

# 🗂 11.2 Making Your Storyboard

A storyboard is composed of **panels**, each containing:

- 🖼️ Thumbnail (drawing, found image, AI, staged photo)  
- 🔢 Shot number  
- 📝 Notes (action, camera movement, framing, transitions)

Programs like **CeltX** can auto-layout your panels.

---

## ✏️ Ways to Get Thumbnails on the Page

### 1️⃣ ✏️ Draw Thumbnails
- Stick figures OK!  
- Show eyelines, movement arrows, blocking, framing.

### 2️⃣ 🔍 Annotate Found Images
Sources:
- Frameset.app  
- Google Images  
- Screenshots  
- Your location photos  

Add circles, arrows, zoom boxes, etc.

### 3️⃣ 🤖 Use AI
AI can assist with inspiration, but **you** control framing and shot meaning.

### 4️⃣ 📸 Stage Photos
Use classmates, objects, or yourself to recreate shot composition.

---

# 🖼️ 11.3 Example Thumbnails for Shot Types

Example from **The Wire** sequence:

- 📷 **1B** – Wide Shot  
- 🎭 **1A** – Medium Single  
- 👥 **2A** – Medium 3-Shot  
- 🚶 **2B** – Medium Single (walking in)  
- 😐 **2C** – Close-Up  
- 👁️ **2D** – OTS Close-Up  
- 👤 **3A** – Medium 2-Shot  
- 😶 **3B** – Close-Up  
- 🎥 **3C** – Dirty Single CU  
- 😑 **3D** – Close-Up  

### Thumbnails must show:

- 📏 Framing  
- ↔️ Subject placement  
- 👀 Eyeline  
- 🖼️ Aspect ratio  
- 🎞️ Camera relationship (OTS, 2-shot, dirty single)

---
