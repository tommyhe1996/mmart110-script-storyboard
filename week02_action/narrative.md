# Week 02 – Narrative from Action Verbs

## Chosen Actions
Build, Search, Run, Fall, Rise

---

## Act 1 – Build
**Protagonist:** Elias, a young inventor  
Elias builds a small flying machine in his attic, convinced it will change his life.  

---

## Act 2 – Search, Run, Fall
Elias searches the city rooftops for a place to test it.  
He runs along the edge of a roof, the wings flapping wildly, before the contraption collapses.  
He falls hard onto the rooftop.  

---

## Act 3 – Rise
Instead of giving up, Elias rises to his feet.  
He stares at the broken machine, sketches new ideas in his notebook, and smiles—ready to try again.
