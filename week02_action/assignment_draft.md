# The Vending Machine  
**A Silent Short Screenplay by Zhonghuang He**

---

## INT. COLLEGE HALLWAY – DAY
A bulletin board full of colorful posters.  

A STUDENT (early 20s, backpack on, headphones in) stops in front of a VENDING MACHINE filled with bright drinks.  

---

## CLOSE ON – STUDENT’S FACE
He stares at the drinks. Thinking.  
Eyes dart from top to bottom row.  

---

## INSERT – VENDING MACHINE
Rows of bottles: sports drinks, sodas, water.  
The prices flash on a small LED screen.  

---

## INT. COLLEGE HALLWAY – CONTINUOUS
The student fumbles in his pocket.  
Pulls out a crumpled bill.  
Tries to feed it into the slot.  

The machine **SPITS** it back out.  

---

## CLOSE ON – THE STUDENT
He sighs, frustrated.  
Smooths the bill against his shirt.  
Tries again.  

This time the machine **ACCEPTS** it.  

---

## INSERT – BUTTONS
The student presses **B-5**.  
A faint **WHIRR** inside the machine.  

---

## WIDE SHOT – VENDING MACHINE
The bottle jiggles in its slot…  
…stops.  

The screen flashes: **NO DRINK.**  

---

## CLOSE ON – STUDENT’S REACTION
He throws his hands up.  
Mouth open in disbelief.  
Half laughing, half annoyed.  

---

## INSERT – COIN RETURN SLOT
The student pushes the refund button.  
Reaches down.  
Pulls out his money.  

---

## WIDE SHOT – HALLWAY
The student walks away shaking his head, clutching the crumpled bill.  
Behind him, the vending machine HUMS quietly, full of drinks but giving none.  

**FADE OUT.**
