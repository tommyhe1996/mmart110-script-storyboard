## 2.2 Create a Story from Action Verbs

**Background**  
- Screenplays are scripts for films, expressing an underlying dramatic narrative.  
- A **narrative** is a “spoken or written account of connected events.”  
- A **screenplay** is a specially formatted document giving acting instructions and scene directions.  
- Writing out a **narrative in prose** often helps before translating it into screenplay format.  

**Activity**  

### Part 1 – Watch and Collect Actions  
- Watch *The Big House*: [YouTube Link](https://youtu.be/x3Cv6oIAaFA?si=q4J7u0TNM4qTX-9d)  
- Note **actions** you observe (verbs: *make, run, wander, stop, build*, etc.).  
- *Trigger warning: rocket fire*  

### Part 2 – Build Your Narrative  
1. **Choose 5 actions** from your list.  
2. **Arrange them into 3 Acts**.  
   - Example:  
     - Act 1 – Build  
     - Act 2 – Experiment, Wander, Run  
     - Act 3 – Stop  
3. **Define your protagonist** (name + identity).  
   - Example: Jolene, a cactus.  
4. **Write a narrative** (beginning, middle, end) using your verbs.  

---

### ✅ Sample Student Narrative  

**Chosen Actions:** Build, Search, Run, Fall, Rise  

**Act 1 – Build**  
Protagonist: **Elias, a young inventor**  
Elias builds a small flying machine in his attic, convinced it will change his life.  

**Act 2 – Search, Run, Fall**  
He searches the city rooftops for a place to test it. When he launches, the machine sputters. He runs along the edge of a roof, the wings breaking apart, and finally falls hard onto the rooftop.  

**Act 3 – Rise**  
Instead of giving up, Elias rises to his feet. He stares at the broken machine, sketches new ideas in his notebook, and smiles—ready to try again.

## 2.3 How to Format an Industry Standard Film Script

**What is Formatting?**  
The way a script is laid out on the page.  

**What is Industry Standard?**  
Agreed-upon professional formatting conventions in the movie industry (Hollywood, studios, production).  

**Why It Matters**  
Proper formatting signals professionalism and training.

### ✅ Sample Screenplay Excerpt (Based on Narrative Above)

TITLE: Flight of Elias
By [Student Name]

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP – DAY

A cluttered attic window bursts open. ELIAS (20s) drags out a strange contraption with canvas wings.

He builds the final piece—a crooked wooden lever—tightening it with a screwdriver.

EXT. ROOFTOPS – CONTINUOUS

Elias searches for a long, flat stretch of roof. He spots one in the distance.

He runs, machine strapped to his back. The wings flap wildly.

Suddenly, the machine collapses. Elias falls hard onto the rooftop.

Silence.

He rises slowly, brushing dust from his clothes. He picks up a torn blueprint, sketches new lines, and smiles faintly.
