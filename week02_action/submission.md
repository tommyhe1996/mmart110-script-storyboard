## Part 1 – Narrative (from Action Verbs)

**Chosen Actions:** Build, Search, Run, Fall, Rise  

**Act 1 – Build**  
Elias, a young inventor, builds a small flying machine in his attic, convinced it will change his life.  

**Act 2 – Search, Run, Fall**  
He searches the city rooftops for a place to test it. When he launches, the machine sputters. He runs along the edge of a roof, the wings breaking apart, and finally falls hard onto the rooftop.  

**Act 3 – Rise**  
Instead of giving up, Elias rises to his feet. He stares at the broken machine, sketches new ideas in his notebook, and smiles—ready to try again.

## Part 2 – Screenplay (Excerpt)

TITLE: Flight of Elias
By [Your Full Name]

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP – DAY

A cluttered attic window bursts open.
ELIAS (20s) drags out a strange contraption with canvas wings.

He builds the final piece—a crooked wooden lever—tightening it with a screwdriver.

EXT. ROOFTOPS – CONTINUOUS

Elias searches for a long, flat stretch of roof.
He spots one in the distance.

He runs, machine strapped to his back.
The wings flap wildly.

Suddenly, the machine collapses.
Elias falls hard onto the rooftop.

Silence.

He rises slowly, brushing dust from his clothes.
He picks up a torn blueprint, sketches new lines, and smiles faintly.

FADE OUT.
