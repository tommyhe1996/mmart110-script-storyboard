## Script

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP – DAY

A cluttered attic window bursts open.
ELIAS (20s) drags out a strange contraption with canvas wings.

He builds the final piece—a crooked wooden lever—tightening it with a screwdriver.

⸻

EXT. ROOFTOPS – CONTINUOUS

Elias searches for a long, flat stretch of roof.
He spots one in the distance.

He runs, machine strapped to his back.
The wings flap wildly.

Suddenly, the machine collapses.
Elias falls hard onto the rooftop.

⸻

SILENCE.

He rises slowly, brushing dust from his clothes.
He picks up a torn blueprint, sketches new lines, and smiles faintly.

FADE OUT.
