# The Vending Machine  
**A Silent Silent Screenplay by Zhonghuang He**

---

## EXT. BERKELEY STADIUM – DAY

A STUDENT runs laps on the hot track.  
His arms pump, his legs pound against the ground.  

**CLOSE ON – SWEAT**  
– Sweat drips down his forehead.  
– Sweat rolls across his cheek.  
– His shirt sticks to his chest.  

The STUDENT slows, gasping for breath.  
He wipes his forehead with his arm.  

**INSERT – HAND TO THROAT**  
He swallows hard, dry and desperate.  

---

## EXT. CAMPUS WALKWAY – DAY

The STUDENT stumbles along the path.  
In the distance – a gleaming **VENDING MACHINE**.  

His eyes light up.  
He drags himself toward it.  

**AERIAL SHOT – STRONG SUNLIGHT**  
The sun blazes down.  
The vending machine glimmers like salvation.  
The STUDENT shields his eyes, his body weak with thirst.  

---

## INT. VENDING MACHINE AREA – DAY

The STUDENT reaches the machine.  
He digs into his pocket, pulling out **CRUMPLED BILLS**.  

**INSERT – MONEY IN HAND**  
The bills are wrinkled, sweaty, barely usable.  

He pushes one into the slot.  
The machine **SPITS** it back out.  

---

## SEQUENCE – STRUGGLE

– He smooths the bill against his shirt.  
– He tries again. The machine **ACCEPTS**.  
– He jabs the buttons, waiting for a response.  
– Nothing happens.  

Frustrated, he **BANGS** the glass with his palm.  
He crouches, peering inside.  
The bottles are lined neatly, mocking him.  

He hits the coin return.  
Nothing.  

---

## CLOSE ON – STUDENT’S FACE

Sweat streams down his temple.  
He exhales sharply, defeated.  

Hopeless, he turns away.  
His shoulders sag as he walks off.  

---

## WIDE SHOT – VENDING MACHINE

**SUDDENLY – A LOUD RATTLE.**  
The vending machine comes alive.  

**INSERT – DRINK TRAY**  
Bottles CRASH down, one after another.  
Water. Soda. Sports drinks.  

The machine **EXPLODES WITH DRINKS** – dozens tumble into a pile.  

---

## CLOSE ON – STUDENT

He spins back around.  
His mouth falls open.  
His eyes blink, empty.  

**BLANK FACE.**  

---

## FINAL SHOT

The STUDENT stands frozen, surrounded by a ridiculous mountain of bottles.  
He does not pick up a single one.  

The sun still beats down.  
The vending machine HUMS quietly, now overflowing.  

**FADE OUT.**
