# Weekly Reflection – MMART 110

**1. What sticks with you?**  
What sticks with me this week is the reminder that *action is the foundation of all film storytelling*. I realized how every script, no matter how complex, is built from visible actions that the audience can see. The way the module broke down **shots, sequences, and scenes** also stayed with me—I now understand better how each is a building block that leads into the next, and how these elements work together to tell a story visually.  

**2. Where did you get confused or lost?**  
I got a little confused when trying to translate my short “action verb narrative” into screenplay format. It was challenging to know exactly how much detail to write in an action line without turning it into prose. I also found myself questioning whether my sluglines were formatted correctly and whether I was following the rules consistently.  

**3. Challenges with this module**  
The biggest challenge was focusing only on *visual storytelling* without relying on dialogue. I am used to thinking in words, so shifting my mindset to actions and images was difficult but also eye-opening. It made me think more like a filmmaker—asking, “How would the audience *see* this on screen?” instead of “How would I *say* this in words?” Even though it was hard, I feel like I grew a lot from this exercise.
