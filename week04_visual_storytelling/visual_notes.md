# Visual Storytelling – Study Notes (Highlights)

## 🎯 Module Goals
- Translate words ↔ images  
- Describe characters & settings in film-specific language  
- Recognize stereotypes vs. deeper character development  
- Use setting to show **mood** (light, sound, weather, etc.)  

---

## 🖼 Visual Storytelling
- Storytelling through pictures = ancient → cave art → storyboards.  
- Narrative picture = characters + recognizable forms + interactions.  
- Sequence of images shows events over time.  
- Not every picture is narrative → abstract = mood/palette inspiration.  

---

## 👤 Character Development
**External (visible):** appearance, age, gender, race, ethnicity.  
**Internal (action-based):** objective, beliefs, personality, status.  

➡️ *Aristotle: “Character is action.”*  

**Avoid:** lazy stereotypes.  
**Do:** give strong, specific objectives.
