# Week 04 – Visual Storytelling Checklist ✅

## Module 4 Focus
- Translate **words ↔ images**  
- Describe **character + setting** in film language  

---

## 📖 Learning Goals
- Recognize storytelling in pictures (storyboards, cave art → film).  
- Use external & internal traits to describe character.  
- Avoid stereotypes, focus on **objectives**.  
- Use setting descriptions to show **mood** (light, sound, weather, etc).  

---

## 📝 Assignments

### 4.4 Character Description (Due Monday, 11:59pm – 10 pts)
- Write ≤40 words describing protagonist.  
- Use formula:  
My character is [external traits].
You might think this makes them [stereotype].
But really they [truer specifics].
They want [objective].

### 4.6 Three Pictures (Due Monday, 11:59pm – 10 pts)
- Find **3 images** that represent your setting.  
- Create a PDF with:  
- The 3 pictures.  
- A short explanation of each (light, composition, feeling).
